Privacy policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Remembrall is a personal application used only by its owner. It reads the owner's own Google Calendar data through Google's APIs, with the owner's explicit consent, solely to display his own schedule back to him.

That data is stored in the owner's private infrastructure, is never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and is accessible to no one but the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Remembrall's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website itself sets no cookies and collects no visitor data.
